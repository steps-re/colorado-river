The instrument that closes it: a federal water option (AMC)

Your proposal, structured. The federal government buys an option from an entity like Metropolitan to hold up to 1 MAF behind Lake Mead. It earns the premium whether or not it is exercised, and it has to be financeable — the feds can count on the water, not "we'll leave it only if we want to."

Term-sheet sketch
  • Underlying: up to 1 MAF held in Lake Mead, verified by lake elevation
  • Buyer: Reclamation (credit-worthy offtaker)
  • Seller: MWD / holder of the conserved water
  • Premium: annual $/AF, paid on the option, not the full volume
  • Trigger: elevation threshold; exercisable, not discretionary
  • Term: 10–20 years (the missing tenor)
Why it is financeable now
  • The strike is observable: lake elevation, not a paper right (leg 1).
  • The price is already set: Reclamation pays MWD $400/AF for conserved water left in Lake Mead today, on a documented board agreement.
  • The proof works: it just needs the durable tenor instead of a one-year buy.
